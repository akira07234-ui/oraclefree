Guanyin Sign No. 92 — 蔡卿报恩 (Caiqing Repays Kindness)

上签戌宫

The Poem

经商得利称心怀。福禄荣华倍获财。若问进身谋望事。秀才一举状元回。

The Meaning

此卦鱼龙变化之象。凡事有贵人助也。

The allusion: 蔡卿报恩 — the poem borrows this episode; read your question against how the story's figure fared.

In English

Theme: Kindness returns in kind.

Advice: A favor you once granted returns amplified. Likewise, generosity now plants tomorrow's harvest.
